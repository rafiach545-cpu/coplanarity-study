# Thesis paragraph — Results of the reproduction (v2)

We reproduced the signal efficiencies of Table I in Knapen et al. (arXiv:1708.02243) with settings classified by their source. The main result uses the acceptance the paper states: only the pixel and SCT barrels, which "effectively restricts the fiducial range to |η| ≲ 1.8". Each of the four independent signal samples is analysed separately.

**Coloured benchmark** (m_Q = 1800 GeV, Λ = 2 keV): the trigger efficiencies (101/419 = 0.241 and 207/873 = 0.237) and fiducial efficiencies (7/27 = 0.26 and 25/68 = 0.37) agree with the paper's 0.24 and 0.28 within Wilson 95% intervals.

**Electroweak benchmark** (m_Q = 800 GeV, Λ = 4 keV): the trigger efficiency is 0.174–0.178, against the paper's 0.10 (p < 10⁻⁶). It stayed at 0.13–0.19 under 36 documented variations of the event generation. The fiducial efficiency with the stated acceptance is 40/89 = 0.45 and 94/186 = 0.51, also well above the paper's 0.28. Only the finite barrel lengths of the reference detector give 0.28 (0.33–0.37), but the paper does not say these were applied.

**Reconstruction** (a reconstructed plane; two hits in seven layers and at least one in the eighth; Δw < 1 cm; Δs < 0.01 cm) depends mainly on the SCT resolution along the beam. The reference detector note gives 580 µm. The paper doubles "the intrinsic accuracy" without naming a coordinate.
- **At 1.16 mm:** EW 6/40 = 0.15 and 21/94 = 0.22; colored 1/7 and 3/25 = 0.12.
- **At 0.58 mm:** EW 0.25–0.31; colored 0.20–0.43.
- **Without smearing along the beam:** EW 0.85 and 0.81 (borderline against 0.72, p ≈ 0.07); colored 0.71 and 0.44 (paper 0.35).

The no-smearing treatment has no documented source, although it matches the paper's own Δs distributions best. We therefore present all three as a systematic envelope rather than choosing one.

**Backgrounds.** No pile-up event (0/5000 at ⟨μ⟩ = 50) and no photon conversion (0/9139) passes the signal region when the SCT is smeared as the reference implies. This agrees with the paper's conclusion. Without smearing along the beam, however, 30 of 9139 conversions (0.33%) pass, so the treatment that brings the electroweak efficiency closest to the paper is not background-free.
