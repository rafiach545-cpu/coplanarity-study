How the angular momentum L of the quirk pair builds up and returns, compared with Appendix A of arXiv:2002.07503. (a) CONCEPTUAL SKETCH, not to scale and not simulation output: pair's rest frame, magnetic field acting as a constant field E* = γ v_cm × B, which pushes +q up and −q down. For 0 < g < 1 quirk 1 is on one side and the torque q d × E* is positive; after the quirks pass at g = 1, d points the other way and the torque has the same size and opposite sign. The sketch shows only this EM torque; the string term of Eq. 3 adds a smaller part (±0.09 of ±0.64 ×10¹² ħ) with the same sign pattern. At g = 0 and g = 2 the sketch shows the relation of Eq. A.5: z₁(2n) = −z₂(2n) = 0 with opposite velocities (head-on, as at production). Eq. A.5 says nothing about the odd meetings; there the quirks pass off-centre with the largest |L|. (b), (c) NUMERICAL SIMULATION of Eq. 3 with the Lorentz force for the event with the paper's kinematics (v* = 0.556, β_cm = 0.0975, charges ±2/3, 3000 RK4 steps per period): d and |L| in the pair's rest frame vs g = r / 1.164 cm (the spacing of the meetings). The simulated |L| is 0.64 ×10¹² ħ at g = 1, 3 and consistent with zero at g = 2, 4 (below 10⁻⁵ of the peak; the remaining value depends on the time step). The agreement of (c) with Eq. A.4–A.5 is the numerical result; the sketch in (a) only illustrates it.
