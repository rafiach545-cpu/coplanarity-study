**GEO5.** 3D view of the full ATLAS Run 2 inner-detector model (used in Stage 2): the same 8 barrel layers, 3 pixel disks and 9 SCT end-cap disks per side (each SCT disk drawn as its outer, middle and inner rings, R3 Table 4.6). Layers are infinitely thin surfaces; no material effects act on the quirk trajectories in either geometry. The TRT is not included (as in Knapen et al.). True scale on all three axes. Semi-transparent surfaces; one quadrant (x < 0, y > 0) is cut away to show the inner layers; the beam axis z is horizontal. Element positions from tables/X06_geometry_elements.csv (from sim/detsim07.build, the simulation geometry); beam pipe from geometry/atlas_run2_id_geometry.csv (R6). Radii: R1 Table 1 (arXiv:2007.07624); lengths: R2 (arXiv:1803.00844), R3 (JINST 3 S08003 ch. 4); disks: R1 Table 1, R3 Tables 4.3, 4.6.
