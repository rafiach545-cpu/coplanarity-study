WHY THIS EVENT DOESN'T MATCH THE PAPER'S NUMBERS EXACTLY

This is the only candidate spanning all 8 layers (≥15 hits) found across all 101 trigger-passing colored-quirk events at this benchmark — not a cherry-picked pass, and specifically not just "smallest Δs regardless of coverage" (a few isolated candidates with tighter Δs exist, but cover only 3–5 of the 8 layers, correctly rejected by the signal-region's per-layer hit-multiplicity requirement). It clears the algorithm's seed cut (Δs < 0.05 cm) comfortably but not the tight final signal-region cut (Δs < 0.01 cm) that the paper's own example event satisfies — across the full corrected sample, zero events pass every signal-region cut at this exact benchmark point.

Part of the gap traces to detector inputs, not a flaw in the physics: this reproduction uses the real, measured ATLAS Run‑2 SCT strip resolution (1.16 mm in z, from the 2020 ID alignment paper, arXiv:2007.07624) rather than the original paper's own 2009 pre‑Run‑2 projected resolution figures. Feeding the exact, unsmeared trajectory through the same fit gives Δs ≈ 5 μm — essentially perfect coplanarity — confirming this part of the gap is detector resolution, not the trajectory integrator or the plane‑fit algorithm.
