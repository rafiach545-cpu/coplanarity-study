READING THIS HONESTLY

This is a shape-only comparison — each curve normalized to its own area, not to a cross-section. With only 21–23 candidates per benchmark, individual bins carry real statistical noise; treat the broad trend (mass concentrated well above the paper's final Δs<0.01cm cut) as the finding, not the exact bin heights.
